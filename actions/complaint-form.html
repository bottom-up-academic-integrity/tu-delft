Report an Incident

By submitting a complaint, you help us document cases of academic complicity and unsafety at TU Delft related to Palestine. Reports will be kept in record, anonymized when needed, and compiled to share with the Works Council, unions, the University Board, Delta, and journalists. We also aim to notify the Integrity Office and Diversity Officers in each faculty, and to make cases visible to the public through our own channels — always protecting anonymity while exposing what is happening. Your contribution strengthens our collective call for accountability and ensures these issues cannot be ignored or silenced.
